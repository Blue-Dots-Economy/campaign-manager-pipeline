INTENT SCORE - HOW IT WORKS
===========================

The score is 0 to 10. It answers one question:

    "Did this person actually want a job?"

0 = no.  10 = yes, badly.
High scores are the people worth calling back.


FIRST WE CHECK FOR 4 SITUATIONS
-------------------------------
If any of these is true, we stop and use that score:

    Never phoned them yet ................. 0
    They applied for a job ................ 9   (best outcome)
    Wanted to apply, our system broke ..... 7   (our fault, not theirs)
    Phone never connected ................. 0


OTHERWISE, ADD UP POINTS
------------------------
    Talked over 10 seconds ....... +1
    Talked over 30 seconds ....... +1
    Talked over 1 minute ......... +1
    Talked over 2 minutes ........ +1

    We showed them real jobs ..... +2   (worth double - they had to
                                         tell us what work they want
                                         and where they live)

    They talked back to us ....... +1

Longer talk = more interest. Max this way is 7.
To beat 7 you have to actually apply (that's the 9).


EXAMPLES
--------
    Talked 4 min, saw jobs, applied ............ 9
    Talked 40 sec, no jobs shown, mostly quiet .. 2


WHERE THE ANSWERS COME FROM
---------------------------
All of it from the phone system's own record of the call.
Nothing is guessed.


NOTE ON OLD SPREADSHEETS
------------------------
An older version gave no points for showing jobs, so old
scores are usually 1 lower. It also produced 8s and 10s.
Ours only gives 0-7 and 9. Ours isn't broken, just different.


IN THE CODE
-----------
transform.py -> compute_intent_score()

Every row also stores why it got its score, in the column
intent_score_reasoning (e.g. ">10s; >30s; jobs shown").
